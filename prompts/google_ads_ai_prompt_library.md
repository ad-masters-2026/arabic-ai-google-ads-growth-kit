# Google Ads AI Prompt Library

## 1. Market and intent research

```text
You are a senior Google Ads strategist for the MENA market.

Business:
[describe business]

Country/city:
[market]

Offer:
[offer]

Landing page:
[paste landing page text or URL summary]

Task:
Generate Google Ads keyword themes in Arabic and English. Classify each theme by search intent:
- high commercial intent
- medium intent
- problem-aware
- informational
- support/login intent
- job intent
- negative keyword candidate

Return a table with:
keyword/theme, intent, suggested match type, campaign, ad group, landing page angle, risk notes.
```

## 2. Arabic negative keyword expansion

```text
Act as a Google Ads search term analyst.

Service:
[service]

Target customer:
[target customer]

Market:
[country]

Here is a list of search terms:
[paste terms]

Task:
Identify terms that should be added as negative keywords. Group them by:
- free intent
- job intent
- learning intent
- support/login intent
- government/platform intent
- unrelated product/service
- low buying intent

Return:
negative keyword, suggested match type, reason in Arabic.
```

## 4. Ad copy generation

```text

Service:
[service]

[keyword theme]

Audience:
[target audience]

[offer]

[professional/direct]

Avoid exaggerated claims. Make the copy suitable for Google Ads.

Return:
15 headlines under 30 characters when possible,
4 long headlines,
4 descriptions,
CTA options,
landing page message match notes.
```
