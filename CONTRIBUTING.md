# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve this project.

## Good contributions

- Arabic negative keyword categories
- Industry-specific Search campaign structures
- Performance Max asset group planning examples
- AI prompts for campaign research, audit, and optimization
- Measurement and conversion tracking QA steps
- Landing page message-match checklists
- Case-study templates without private client data

## Contribution rules

1. Do not include client names, private account data, screenshots, emails, or personal data.
2. Do not submit copyrighted course material or paid templates copied from others.
3. Use clear Arabic or English.
4. When adding negative keywords, include the reason/category.
5. For examples, use fictional business names and fictional numbers.
6. Keep recommendations practical and testable.

## Pull request checklist

- [ ] I removed private or client-specific data.
- [ ] I added the content to the correct folder.
- [ ] I explained why this is useful.
- [ ] I checked spelling and formatting.
