# AI Google Ads Operating System

## Goal

Create a repeatable workflow where AI supports the media buyer across research, campaign structure, creative messaging, conversion quality, optimization, and scaling.

## The operating loop

### 1. Business and offer understanding

Before keywords or campaigns, define:

- What is the product/service?
- Who is the decision maker?
- What pain does the offer solve?
- What is the strongest proof?
- What is the target city/country?
- What is the expected conversion?
- What is a qualified lead?
- What is the sales cycle?
- What is the acceptable CPA/CPL/ROAS?

### 2. Search intent mapping

Classify queries by intent:

| Intent | Example | Campaign Action |
|---|---|---|
| High intent | "شركة إدارة حملات جوجل ادز" | Exact/Phrase Search |
| Comparison | "أفضل شركة تسويق إلكتروني" | Search + landing proof |
| Problem aware | "كيف أزيد مبيعات المتجر" | Search/YouTube content |
| Low intent | "تعلم جوجل ادز مجانا" | Exclude or separate content campaign |
| Support/job intent | "وظائف جوجل ادز" | Negative keyword |

### 3. AI-assisted keyword expansion

Use AI to generate:

- Arabic variations
- English variations
- Local dialect variations
- Misspellings
- Problem-based keywords
- Competitor-alternative keywords
- Negative keyword candidates

AI output must always be reviewed by a human before upload.

### 4. Campaign structure

Recommended Search structure:

- Brand campaign
- Core commercial intent campaign
- Service/category campaigns
- Competitor or alternatives campaign
- Arabic-English split when volume is enough
- Experiment campaign for new hypotheses

### 5. Landing page message match

The landing page must reflect:

- Main keyword theme
- Main promise
- Service category
- Geographic relevance
- Proof elements
- Clear CTA
- Trust signals

### 6. Measurement quality

Do not scale before verifying:

- Primary conversions are correct
- Duplicate conversions are avoided
- Calls/forms/WhatsApp events are tracked correctly
- Lead quality feedback exists
- CRM or sheet status is updated
- Offline conversion import is considered when possible

### 7. Optimization rhythm

Daily:

- Spend anomalies
- Conversion tracking issues
- Search terms that waste budget
- Disapproved ads or assets

Weekly:

- Query mining
- Budget reallocation
- Lead quality review
- Ad copy testing
- Landing page friction review

Monthly:

- Structure rebuild if needed
- New campaign experiments
- Scaling decision
- Conversion model review
- Creative refresh

### 8. Scaling framework

Scale only when:

- Conversion tracking is stable
- CPA or ROAS is within target range
- Lead quality is acceptable
- Search terms are clean
- Impression share or budget loss suggests room
- Landing page conversion rate is not broken

Scaling methods:

- Increase budget gradually
- Expand keyword coverage
- Add location layers
- Add asset groups
- Improve conversion value signals
- Use offline conversion quality data
- Add experiments before major changes
